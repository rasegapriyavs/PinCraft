Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

PinCraft is a personal web application for preparing Pinterest Pins. This policy explains what information the application currently handles and how that may change when optional Pinterest publishing features are introduced.

Information PinCraft may process

When you use PinCraft, you may provide an image, Pin title, description, destination URL, and, in a future version, a Pinterest board selection. In the current version, this information is used only in your browser to display the form and image preview. PinCraft does not currently provide account sign-in, direct publishing, or a server-side database.

How information is used

Information you enter is used to help you prepare a Pin. A selected image is read locally by your browser so that a preview can be displayed. The current application does not send the image or form contents to PinCraft servers, store them in a PinCraft account, or publish them to Pinterest.

Pinterest API and future publishing

PinCraft may later offer the option to publish Pins to your own Pinterest account using the official Pinterest API and Pinterest OAuth. If you choose to connect your account, PinCraft may request only the permissions needed to create Pins and access the boards required for that feature. PinCraft will use that access only to provide the publishing functionality you request.

Before this feature is available, PinCraft will update this policy to describe the exact Pinterest permissions, information flows, and storage practices that apply.

Authentication and access tokens

The current application does not authenticate with Pinterest and does not collect, transmit, or store Pinterest access tokens. If OAuth is introduced in the future, access tokens will be handled only as necessary to provide the connected Pinterest feature and protected using appropriate safeguards. You will be able to disconnect your Pinterest account through the available connection controls or by revoking access in Pinterest.

Data storage and retention

PinCraft does not currently maintain a server-side store of the images or Pin details you enter. Content stays in your active browser session unless your browser itself retains it. If future features require storage, PinCraft will explain what is stored, why it is needed, and how long it is retained before those features are released.

Third-party services

PinCraft is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under its own privacy policy when you visit the site. If Pinterest publishing is enabled later, Pinterest will process information according to Pinterest's own terms and privacy policy. PinCraft does not currently include third-party advertising or analytics services.

Security

PinCraft is designed to minimize the information it handles. No method of online transmission or storage is completely secure, but reasonable safeguards will be used for any future connected-account features. Please do not enter credentials, access tokens, or other sensitive information into PinCraft forms unless a clearly identified, secure feature specifically asks for it.

Your choices and rights

You may choose not to provide optional Pin details or images. Because the current version does not store personal data on PinCraft servers, there is currently no PinCraft account data to access, correct, export, or delete. If future data storage is introduced, this policy will explain how to exercise applicable privacy rights.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as PinCraft develops, particularly before Pinterest connection and publishing features become available. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
